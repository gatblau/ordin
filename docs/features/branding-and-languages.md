# Branding, languages and accessibility

**ordin can carry your company's brand, speaks your language and is designed to be accessible.**

Category: [Open and adaptable](README.md#open-and-adaptable) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- The name, logo, colours and fonts can all be rebranded. Approval cards and safety cues stay fixed.
- Translatable from day one, including right-to-left languages. Ordin talks in your language.
- Dictation processed on your computer.
- Designed to meet accessibility guidelines.
