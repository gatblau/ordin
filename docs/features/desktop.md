# Desktop app

**Where you work with ordin day to day, on your own computer.**

Category: [Where it runs](README.md#where-it-runs) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- The full app: conversations, workspaces, documents, agents and approvals.
- Your own [ordin servers](own-server.md) shown next to your local workspaces.
- A way to ask a [hosted company agent](hosted-agent.md) for help and answer its approval requests.

## How it works

1. Install ordin in your own user account. No administrator rights are needed.
2. Sign in with accounts you already have, such as your AI engine.
3. ordin installs, checks and repairs what it needs by itself.

## You stay in charge

- The desktop works for one person and takes no requests from outside.
- Nothing starts on a timer. Work starts when you ask.

## Good to know

- Every program ordin brings is signed by its publisher, so IT can allow it with one rule.
- When ordin can't finish a step, it says in one plain sentence what you can do.
