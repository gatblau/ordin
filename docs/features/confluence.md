# Confluence

**Read Confluence pages, give them to agents, and publish your documents as pages.**

Category: [Documents](README.md#documents) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- Browse and read Confluence pages, and use them as inputs for agents.
- Publish local documents: text becomes a page, other files are attached as they are.
- A Confluence page can be a published copy of a [Markdown original](markdown.md) and stay up to date with it.

## How it works

1. Sign in with your own Confluence account. Your company's profile names the site.
2. Link a Confluence location to a workspace or folder and approve the link once.
3. Updates to files already published are sent automatically. New or removed files wait in a list for a quick check.

## You stay in charge

- An edit made in Confluence is shown to you and can be brought back as a copy. It never overwrites a local file.

## Good to know

- Confluence is not offered on the [ordin server](own-server.md).
