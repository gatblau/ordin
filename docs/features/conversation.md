# Talking to Ordin

**One conversation per piece of work. You say what you need, and Ordin gets the right agents to do it.**

Category: [Everyday use](README.md#everyday-use) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- One conversation per workspace, typed or dictated.
- Ordin picks the agent for the job, or forms a small team of agents, and keeps them on track.
- Replies are short and plain, with a recommendation when you need to decide something.
- Progress reads plainly: what is happening now and "step 2 of 4", never an invented percentage.

## How it works

1. You say what you want done, in your own words.
2. Ordin hands the work to an agent, or to a [crew](crews.md), and answers the agents' routine questions itself.
3. Ordin checks the result against what you asked for, and only then reports it done.

## You choose

- Its tone (friendly, neutral, warm or direct), how formal it is and how short its replies are.
- The name it calls you, and short notes on how you like things explained.
- Tone never changes approval cards, errors or facts.

## Good to know

- Screens are put together for the step you are on, so there is no menu of features to learn.
- Dictation is processed on your computer, and works where the operating system provides it (macOS 26 and later).
