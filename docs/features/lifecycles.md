# Lifecycles

**A lifecycle sets out the stages of a piece of work: who does each one, which agent helps, and what must be done before it moves on.**

Category: [Teamwork](README.md#teamwork) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- A path of stages with "You are here" and the handovers at each stage.
- Choices: a stage can lead to different next stages, such as extra reviews for a high-risk supplier, with Ordin's recommendation beside each.
- Stages side by side, such as security and legal reviewing at once.
- Send-back loops, where a review returns work with a reason, up to a limit on rounds.
- **Show as diagram** draws the lifecycle as a flow chart.

## How it works

1. Start a workspace for your stage in one step, or make an existing workspace follow a stage.
2. The workspace offers the stage's agent and shows what you must hand on.
3. A later stage starts when every stage under way has handed over.

## You stay in charge

- Past the limit on rounds the choice stays yours, and the record shows the extra round.

## Good to know

- Lifecycles live in [agent repositories](agent-repositories.md). Curators change them in a lifecycle draft, and ordin checks it before it goes for review.
