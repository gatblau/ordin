# Meeting minutes

**Record a meeting, or start from a recording or transcript you already have, and get minutes you can edit, export and talk to Ordin about.**

Category: [Everyday use](README.md#everyday-use) · Status: Built, not yet released

## What you get

- Minutes of a meeting, written by the AI service your agents use and kept in the workspace.
- Record with OBS Studio, or make minutes from a file: `.vtt`, `.docx` or `.txt` transcripts, or `.m4a`, `.mp3` and `.wav` recordings.
- Ordin can read the minutes of a workspace in conversation, and turn a recording into text when you ask it to.

## How it works

1. In the tree, open the ⋯ or right-click menu of **Minutes** under a workspace and choose **Record…** or **Make minutes from a file…**.
2. While the work runs, its row under **Minutes** shows how far it has got. Its menu has **Cancel**, and **Try again** if it failed.
3. The finished minutes appear as a row under **Minutes**. Open one to read or edit it; its menu exports it as PDF or Word, or deletes it.

## You stay in charge

- Every time you record, Ordin first reminds you to tell everyone in the meeting.
- Speech is turned into text on your computer. The recording never leaves it.
- Before the first minutes are written, Ordin asks once: only the text of the meeting goes to the AI service your agents already use.
- The speech model is downloaded only after you agree, and *Needs you* lists anything waiting for your answer.

## Good to know

- Ordin picks the speech model that suits your computer: the larger one on a capable machine, the smaller one otherwise. You can choose another in **Settings › Meeting minutes**.
- Recording needs OBS Studio. If OBS isn't ready, **Record…** says what is wrong and offers the fix.
