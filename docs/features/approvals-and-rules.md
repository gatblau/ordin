# Approvals and rules

**Every action that reaches other people or can't be undone waits for you. Rules are enforced by fixed code, not by an AI model.**

Category: [Trust and safety](README.md#trust-and-safety) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- An approval card for every outside or irreversible action: what it is, where it goes and what it affects.
- Limits on a crew, what an agent may touch and what needs your approval.
- A list, when an agent starts, of anything ordin won't carry across or enforce.

## How it works

1. An agent proposes an action.
2. ordin's own code checks it against the rules.
3. You approve or reject it. The approval card is built by ordin, never by an agent.

## You stay in charge

- Agents reach only the folders and links you have given them.
- Nothing is dropped silently.
