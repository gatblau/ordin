# Workspaces

**A workspace is a named home for one piece of work, such as "Q3 supplier review".**

Category: [Everyday use](README.md#everyday-use) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- Everything for one piece of work in one place: files, linked documents, agents, conversations and results.
- A tree that shows your documents, code and handovers, with documents someone else changed marked until you look.
- Each document opens on a short page: what it is, its state, **Open in [app]** and **Ask Ordin about this**.

## How it works

1. Create a workspace by giving it a name.
2. Drag files and folders in, or ask Ordin to link documents where they live.
3. Agents started in the workspace reach only what it links, at the access you gave.

## You stay in charge

- Agents never reach folders or links you have not given them.
- Every change is a version, and removed files go to the system bin.

## Good to know

- A workspace handles places of up to 100,000 files and 20 folder levels.
