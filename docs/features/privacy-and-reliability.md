# Privacy and reliability

**Your data stays on the machine that runs ordin, and a restart loses nothing and repeats nothing.**

Category: [Trust and safety](README.md#trust-and-safety) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- ordin never asks for, reads or stores your AI account's password or key. Each AI engine signs you in its own way.
- Memory and history stay on your computer or your own server. Nothing runs in a vendor's cloud apart from the AI model itself.
- A full history of runs, approvals and results that survives a restart.

## How it works

1. Every run, approval and result is recorded as it happens.
2. After a restart, ordin resumes from the record.
3. No outside action that already completed runs twice.

## Good to know

- ordin tries to repair a problem before it shows an error. Any error it shows is one plain sentence with one next step.
