# Telegram

**Talk to your ordin server from Telegram, and let agents read groups and draft posts.**

Category: [Messaging](README.md#messaging) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- A chat with your own [ordin server](own-server.md): talk to Ordin, see what needs you and approve from the chat.
- Agents read the groups and channels a workspace links, save their files and draft posts.

## How it works

1. Connect the bot on one guided screen.
2. Link the groups and channels a workspace should read.
3. Approve each post in your own chat with ordin before it goes out.

## You stay in charge

- Every post needs your approval of the exact text.
- Posts come from the bot, never from your own account.
- Your own chat with ordin is never open to agents.

## Good to know

- On the desktop, the Telegram tools work when the Telegram app is installed, and messages arrive while ordin is open.
