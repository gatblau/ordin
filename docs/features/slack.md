# Slack

**Talk to your ordin, reach hosted agents and tell colleagues about handovers, in Slack.**

Category: [Messaging](README.md#messaging) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- Reach agents that run on a server, through Slack. ordin posts as you in a thread and reads the replies.
- Talk to your own [ordin server](own-server.md) from Slack, with the same approvals as on the desktop.
- Announce a [handover](handovers.md) in a direct message or the stage's channel, with a link that opens it in the receiver's ordin.
- Ordin can read the Slack channels a workspace links and answer there.

## How it works

1. Link a Slack channel to a workspace.
2. Ordin reads it and drafts answers or posts.
3. Each post goes out after your approval, or under a narrow rule you set once.

## You stay in charge

- A rule covers only handover notices of one stage, or Ordin's answers in one linked channel, with a daily limit.
- On your own server, a phrase in a linked channel can start a saved workflow, only through a trigger you turn on.

## Good to know

- The server connects to Slack without opening a port.
