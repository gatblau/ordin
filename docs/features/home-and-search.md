# Home and search

**See what needs you across all your workspaces, and find anything from one search box.**

Category: [Everyday use](README.md#everyday-use) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- **Home** shows what is waiting for you, what is running and what finished or changed, in every workspace.
- **Search or go to…** (Ctrl+K, ⌘K on a Mac) finds things across your workspaces, opens them and starts actions.
- **Run again** repeats finished work with the same inputs, and names any newer versions before it starts.

## How it works

1. Open ordin and Home lists what needs you.
2. Pick an item to go straight to it, or search for it.

## Good to know

- Search is for you only. Agents still search only the workspace they work in.
- Home and search are built by ordin's own code and decide nothing on your behalf.
