# Office, PDF and media files

**Agents change files of many kinds through the ArtCraft apps. You open them in your own apps with one click.**

Category: [Documents](README.md#documents) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- Agents change Word documents through WordCraft, PowerPoint slides through DeckCraft, PDFs through PdfCraft and images through PhotoCraft.
- GridCraft makes new spreadsheets, for example when you save a report.
- Other ArtCraft apps create and edit video, audio, drawings and page layouts.
- Word, Excel and PowerPoint files open in your own Microsoft apps with one click.

## How it works

1. Ask for a change to a file.
2. The agent works through the matching ArtCraft app.
3. ordin installs and updates the apps itself. You never install one.

## Good to know

- Agents don't change spreadsheets yet.
- Word and PowerPoint editing grows as the ArtCraft apps gain support for each format.
