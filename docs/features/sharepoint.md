# SharePoint

**Link SharePoint documents where they live. Agents change them, and changes go back only after you approve.**

Category: [Documents](README.md#documents) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- SharePoint documents, typically Word and PowerPoint, linked into a workspace.
- Word, PDF and slides copies in SharePoint that follow a [Markdown original](markdown.md).
- A SharePoint folder can carry a [handover](handovers.md) to the next person.

## How it works

1. Ask Ordin to add a SharePoint location to your workspace and sign in with your own account.
2. Agents read the documents and propose changes.
3. You approve the changes, grouped per request, and ordin sends them back.

## You stay in charge

- Nothing goes back until you approve it.
- ordin first checks that nobody changed the original in the meantime.

## Good to know

- On the [ordin server](own-server.md), SharePoint is not offered where your company blocks the sign-in method a server uses.
