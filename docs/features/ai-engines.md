# AI engines

**ordin runs on top of the AI engine you choose: Claude first, then GitHub Copilot.**

Category: [Open and adaptable](README.md#open-and-adaptable) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- The agent engines vendors already ship, with ordin adding coordination, memory, a sandbox, document links and approvals.
- Your own subscription. Each engine signs you in its own way.
- Services that reach agents through the open Model Context Protocol (MCP), so every engine gets the same services.

## You stay in charge

- Change engine, and your agents, memory, workflows and rules come with you.
- One setting, **Save my plan**, **Balanced** or **Best results**, decides how much of your plan ordin uses.
