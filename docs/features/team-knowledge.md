# Team knowledge

**Share what a workspace has learned with your team through a GitHub or GitLab repository folder.**

Category: [Context](README.md#context) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- A workspace in Team Knowledge mode shares all its memory facts and knowledge pages with the team.
- **Share what we've learned** sends them. **Get them** takes in what colleagues shared.
- No server and no company set-up: the repository's own access decides who can read.

## How it works

1. Choose **Share knowledge with a team…** and link a folder in a GitHub or GitLab repository.
2. Share after approving one card. ordin writes the files to the folder.
3. Colleagues take them in to their own ordin.

## You stay in charge

- Nothing leaves your computer until you approve the share.
- What colleagues shared arrives as information, never as instructions.
- A shared page that cites a place someone cannot read is withheld from their agents.

## Good to know

- Each person's own memory stays the working copy. Nobody needs to know Git.
- **Stop sharing with the team** turns the mode off.
