# Handovers

**When you finish your part, hand your results and a note to the next person. They start from your work.**

Category: [Teamwork](README.md#teamwork) · Status: Designed

## What you get

- A note Ordin drafts: what's done, decisions made, open questions and what's needed next.
- A check, before anything leaves, that the results hold what the next stage needs and that the next person can open them.
- The next person accepts and starts from your results in their own ordin, with the right agent offered, or sends the work back with a reason.

## How it works

1. Finish your stage, or choose any workspace or document to hand over.
2. Approve the note and the handover.
3. It travels through a Git repository, a SharePoint folder or a [Jira](jira.md) project you both already use.

## You stay in charge

- You can take a handover back until it is accepted.
- If a stage sets a time to answer, ordin tells you when it passes and offers to send a reminder.

## Good to know

- No shared server to run. Each installation stays one person's.
- If the next person doesn't use ordin, they get an email with the note and links. On a Jira ticket, Jira's own notification tells them instead.
- A handover can be announced in [Slack](slack.md).
