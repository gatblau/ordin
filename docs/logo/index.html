Clear space and minimum size

Keep the shaded area free of text, other logos and the edge of the window or page. Clear space is measured from the edge of the artwork box in each SVG file, so it can be applied without measuring the drawing.

  • Lockup and logotype: X is half the height of the letter o. In the lockup this equals a quarter of the logo's height, so one measure covers both.
  • Logo alone: X is a quarter of the logo's height: 4px around a 16px logo, 16px around a 64px logo.
  • App icons are exempt. Their tile already carries the platform's own margin.
  • Clear space is a minimum. More is always allowed.

  • The colours are the interface concept's tokens: accent #1F4FB8 in light, #7FA6F5 in dark. No amber or red, which mean "waiting" and "danger".
  • The logotype is drawn as outlines, so it looks the same on every computer and needs no font file. It shares the mark's round geometry: the bowls of o and d, the arches of r and n, and a round dot on the i like the crew.
  • The logo is not mirrored for right-to-left layouts. The shorter second line suggests left-to-right text, which is the usual convention for a document icon.
