| Act in Slack as the person only with their consent, and within the workspace's app approval. | Slack workspace administration; the user's decision of January 2026. | The person signs in to Slack themselves. | Personal data in documents, Slack messages and agent memory. | Organisation's data-protection obligations (to be named, OQ-10). | Data stays on the person's computer, in agent folders and the person's places, except what a run sends to Copilot, wrightrun or Slack, and what the person publishes to Confluence (ADR-026). | Email: personal data of people inside and outside the company, and sending as the person (revision 0.15.0; ADR-070, ADR-071). | The organisation's data-protection obligations (OQ-10) and its email policy; Microsoft's and Google's terms for delegated access. | Only messages matching the workspace's email links are read, and only by Ordin and agents the person granted; messages stay in the mailbox, and what a session read is sent to its model vendor as any document is. IT can turn email off, allow recipients inside the company only, cap recipients and keep named places out of attachments. *With the company's mail server (revision 0.16.0, ADR-071):* connections are encrypted only; IT names the servers; a mail password, where single sign-on is not accepted, is kept only in the OS keychain and removed on **Sign out of email**. |
